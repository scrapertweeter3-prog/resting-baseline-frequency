A resting baseline is 365 samples, not one

A resting heart-rate number means almost nothing on its own and a great deal against your own history, which makes it a frequency problem before it is a physiology problem. The cheapest protocol in biohacking is already running on the wrist: one reading every morning, taken between waking and the first coffee while the body is still horizontal. Kept daily, that habit banks 365 samples a year. Kept occasionally, it banks trivia. The review method at HackedSelf is to price a protocol before judging it, and this one prices at thirty seconds a day.

The calendar does most of the work. A daily sample collects 365 points a year where a weekly habit collects 52, so one year of daily sampling holds as many readings as seven years of weekly sampling. Nothing about the sensor changes and no new gear ships; the whole gain is showing up at the same minute every day.

Any single morning can run hot or cold, which is why the number worth logging is the weekly average. Averaging seven readings narrows the scatter of a single reading by a factor of about 2.6, the square root of seven, straight from sampling arithmetic rather than any study. The same average recomputed every Monday turns 365 noisy points into 52 smooth ones, and drift becomes legible at the week scale instead of the morning scale.

Streak mechanics matter less than coverage. Thirty-five missed mornings out of 365 still leaves 330 samples, about 90 percent of the year and a perfectly usable baseline. The quiet failure is not a missed day but a moved reading: taken after the coffee instead of before it, after the commute instead of in bed, and the sample joins a different population. Same wrist, different protocol.

The payoff arrives when an intervention starts. A new supplement stack, a shifted bedtime, a block of easy aerobic work: the question is always whether resting rate moved, and that question is only answerable against a pre-period. Starting the morning sample on day zero of the intervention leaves nothing to compare against; starting it in October leaves the whole winter as a control.
